Project Overview

Abstract

Anemia is a global health issue, and its diagnosis traditionally relies on manual inspection of peripheral blood smears, a process that is time-consuming, labor-intensive, and prone to human error. This project introduces an automated system for anemia classification using deep learning to overcome these limitations. We employ a hybrid model that leverages the powerful feature extraction capabilities of the InceptionV3 convolutional neural network via transfer learning, coupled with the efficiency and high performance of the LightGBM gradient boosting framework for classification. The system analyzes digital blood smear images to accurately differentiate between anemic and normal samples, demonstrating a significant potential to enhance diagnostic workflows in clinical settings by providing rapid, reliable, and objective results.

Understanding Anemia

A Lack of Healthy Red Blood Cells

Anemia is a medical condition where the blood lacks sufficient healthy red blood cells or hemoglobin. Hemoglobin is the protein in red blood cells that binds and transports oxygen. Without enough oxygen, the body's cells cannot function properly, leading to fatigue and other health issues.

Common Causes of Anemia

  • Iron Deficiency: The most common cause, where the body lacks iron to produce hemoglobin.
  • Vitamin Deficiencies: Lack of folate and vitamin B-12 affects red blood cell production.
  • Chronic Diseases: Conditions like kidney disease, cancer, or autoimmune disorders can interfere.
  • Blood Loss: Significant blood loss from injury, surgery, or internal bleeding.

Signs and Symptoms

Symptoms can vary depending on severity but often include:

  • Fatigue and weakness
  • Pale or yellowish skin
  • Shortness of breath
  • Dizziness
  • Cold hands and feet
  • Headaches

Detailed Methodology

Our system follows a sophisticated pipeline to ensure accurate classification.

01

Image Pre-processing

Each blood smear image is resized to a uniform 331x331 pixels and its pixel values are normalized. This standardization is crucial for consistent model input and performance.

02

Feature Extraction

We utilize InceptionV3, a state-of-the-art CNN, to analyze the images. It extracts a high-dimensional vector of features, capturing complex patterns like cell shape, size, and color.

03

Transfer Learning

The InceptionV3 model was pre-trained on the vast ImageNet dataset. We apply transfer learning to adapt this powerful, generalized knowledge specifically for blood cell analysis.

04

LGBM Classification

The extracted features are fed into a LightGBM classifier. This fast and efficient model makes the final, highly accurate prediction of 'Anemia' or 'Normal'.
